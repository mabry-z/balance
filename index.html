How to play

Place the weights so every row and every column adds up to the target.

  • Tap a weight in the tray, then an empty square.
  • Tap a placed weight to send it back.
  • A line's total turns green when it's full and on target.
43512 628

Every weight gets used, and there's exactly one solution. Each placement is a move. A perfect solve places every weight once.

Your share card shows how many tries each square took, so no two look alike.

A new Balance arrives every day at midnight. Monday is gentle. By Sunday it's 5×5 with repeated weights.

Also try Sequence: put five things in order in four guesses.
